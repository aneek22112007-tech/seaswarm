🌊 SeaSwarm

Agents propose. Humans dispose.

A Switch-powered room where three AI agents and named humans co-run a fishing-boat search and support the family, in coastal Maharashtra. Nothing irreversible leaves the room until a named person signs off in the thread.

The problem

Fishing villages already coordinate in Telegram and WhatsApp groups. When a boat is overdue, that chat fails: the last-known position is buried in scrollback, nobody knows who searched which stretch, and there is no record of who approved the Coast Guard call. SeaSwarm turns the same room into a governed, auditable operation.

Three agents, one room

🧭

Context

Finds the boat in room history and the register, adds wind, sea state and tide, computes a drift cone and daylight left, and posts a one-screen summary.

🗺️

Options

Splits the cone into sectors and posts exactly three plans with coverage, time, fuel, INR cost, daylight margin and risk flags. Re-plans on "Sector-1 clear" and refuses two boats in one sector.

🛟

Action

Stores the Coast Guard / marine police escalation as a pending approval, matches the family to welfare and insurance schemes, pins the operation checklist and records evidence.

🔒

Human gate

Escalation, spending, outside messages, scheme outreach and closing the search wait for Approved — <action> from an authorised role. The wrong role is refused on screen.

Replay: Sagar-4, overdue off Harnai

3 crew, out of Harnai, Ratnagiri. Overdue 2 hours, last radio fix near a reef 6 km offshore, 4 hours of daylight left. Room clock 15 Jan 2026, 14:30 IST. The plan figures below come from the repo's core/ functions.

The kill switch

Same two planning passes, with and without room persistence (make killswitch, logs from logs/).

PERSISTENCE=on
sectors_after_two_passes=3
sector_rows=Sector-1 x1, Sector-2 x1, Sector-3 x1
stored_approvals=1
approver=ramesh
sign-off reply: ramesh (community_leader) approved escalation.
PERSISTENCE=off
sectors_after_two_passes=6
sector_rows=Sector-1 x2, Sector-2 x2, Sector-3 x2
stored_approvals=0
approver=(none)
sign-off reply: No pending approval for escalation.
The sign-off was not applied.

Remove the room and the operation collapses. That is why Switch is the product.

Run it

git clone https://github.com/aneek22112007-tech/seaswarm && cd seaswarm
make up     # Postgres, Mattermost, Switch, 3 agents
make demo   # seed + replay Sagar-4 in #sar-harnai
make audit  # Markdown + PDF audit export

Mattermost on :8065, Switch console on :8090. Single-VPS deploy with Docker + Caddy: deploy/VPS.md.
