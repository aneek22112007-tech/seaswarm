The group chat fails when it matters most.

Sagar-4, three crew, out of Harnai. Overdue two hours. Last radio fix near a reef 6 km offshore. Four hours of daylight left.

In a normal chat the position is buried in scrollback, two boats search the same stretch, and nobody can say who approved the Coast Guard call.

Three agents, one room

Context. Options. Action.

🧭 Context — last fix, crew, heading, wind, sea state and tide → a drift cone and the daylight left.
🗺️ Options — splits the cone into sectors, posts exactly three plans: coverage, time, fuel, INR cost, daylight margin, risk. Never two boats in one sector.
🛟 Action — drafts the Coast Guard escalation as pending, matches the family to welfare schemes, pins the checklist, logs evidence.

Option A: Meerabai → Sector-2 · 1.45 h · 4.1 L · INR 389.50. Every figure comes from pure functions in core/.
